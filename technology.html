What is Timera

More time.
More life.
More you.

Timera is a biocompatible implant designed to collect and store unused time generated by your body’s natural rhythm.

The implant recognizes only its owner and protects stored time until you decide to use it. It is personal, secure and designed to work with your own rhythm.
