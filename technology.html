What is Timera

More time.More life.More you.

Timera is an biocompatible implant where magic and technology meet. It lets you store time during moments when you need it the least, and activate that saved time when you need it the most.

It works like a personal time‑reserve that you control. The implant recognizes only its owner and protects stored time until you decide to use it.
